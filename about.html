Our Story

FoodGrid AI was founded by a group of technologists, logistics specialists, and sustainability advocates who saw a stark gap between food availability and food access.

We believe technology can be a powerful force for good — that's why we built a platform that leverages AI, real-time data, and dynamic routing to create a smarter, faster, and more transparent food redistribution ecosystem.
