About the numbers on this page

FoodGrid AI is currently presented as a proposed system and research prototype. The platform-wide figures shown above (kg saved, meals delivered, people reached) represent target/simulated metrics for demonstration purposes, not measured real-world results — consistent with our own research methodology, which requires that performance figures only be reported as results once produced by an actual experiment or field deployment.

🌍

The Problem, Evidenced

UNEP's Food Waste Index 2024 estimates 1.05 billion tonnes of food were wasted in 2022 — about one-fifth of food available to consumers — while food loss and waste is linked to 8–10% of global greenhouse-gas emissions.

🔬

Our Research Question

Can an AI-assisted coordination system improve the speed and efficiency with which surplus food is matched to eligible recipients, while respecting food-safety and logistics constraints?

⚖️

What We Won't Claim

We will not claim FoodGrid AI is the first food-rescue platform, that AI alone solves hunger, or that simulated figures represent real-world impact before field validation.
