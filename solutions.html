The Existing Landscape

Food redistribution is an established field. FoodGrid AI is designed to add a coordination layer, not to claim to be first.

🛍️

Too Good To Go

A large consumer surplus-food marketplace plus business software, including near-expiry management and AI-powered discounting.

🤝

OLIO

Hyper-local redistribution via trained volunteers — its Food Waste Heroes programme fills gaps that charity collection can't cover.

🇺🇸

Feeding America / MealConnect

A large U.S. charitable food network with a digital donation platform connecting businesses and food banks at scale.

☁️

FoodCloud / Foodiverse

Direct retailer-to-charity matching with regional hubs and refrigerated collection — the closest existing benchmark to FoodGrid's proposed architecture.

🎯
Our research gap

Existing systems demonstrate that digital surplus-food redistribution is feasible. FoodGrid AI's research direction is to evaluate an integrated, multi-stakeholder optimization model that jointly considers supply, demand, perishability, transport capacity, geographic constraints, and measurable impact — rather than claiming to be a first-of-its-kind connector.
